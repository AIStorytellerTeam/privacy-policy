# Account and Data Deletion

**App:** AI Storyteller / Fairytale Gardens  
**Google Play package:** com.unil.aistoryteller  
**Data controller:** Turlaeva Elena Vladimirovna, Republic of Moldova  
**Contact:** aistoryteller.team@gmail.com

## 1. Deleting your account inside the app
This is the fastest way — deletion happens immediately.

- Open the app and sign in to your account.
- Go to the **"Parents"** section (enter your PIN if one is set).
- Open **"Settings" → "Reset data"**.
- Choose **"Delete all data"** if you are the family owner, or **"Delete only
  my data"** if you joined a family with an invite code.
- Confirm. The family owner is asked twice and shown how many child profiles and
  relatives' accounts will be deleted. Once confirmed, the action cannot be undone.

## 2. Deleting your account without the app
If you have already uninstalled the app, email us from the address your account
is registered to:

**aistoryteller.team@gmail.com**, subject line "Account deletion".

We reply within 30 days. We cannot act on a request sent from a different
address — it is the only way available to us to confirm the request comes from
you and not from someone else.

## 3. What is deleted
Permanently and completely:

- the adult account and sign-in record (email, Google link);
- child profiles: name, date of birth, gender, avatar, selected skills, progress;
- all created stories — texts, illustrations and narration files;
- bookmarks, favourites and reading history;
- family settings, invite code, the parent-section PIN;
- the recorded voice sample, if there was one.

If you are the family owner and chose "Delete all data", the accounts of
relatives who joined your family with the invite code are deleted as well.

## 4. What is kept, and for how long
- **On-device data** — sign-in tokens, the PIN hash, settings and the
  illustration cache — is removed when you uninstall the app or reset data.
- **Database backups** held by our hosting provider (Supabase) are kept for a
  limited period and overwritten on schedule. Backups made before your deletion
  expire with them — no later than 30 days.
- **Payment records** (the fact and amount of a purchase, not story content) are
  kept for as long as tax and accounting law requires. We are not permitted to
  delete these on request.
- **Anonymised technical logs** (generation time, success/failure status, no
  names and no content) may be retained for diagnostics; after your account is
  deleted they cannot be linked back to you.

## 5. Your other rights
Besides deletion you may request a copy of your data, correction of inaccuracies
or restriction of processing. Write to the same address —
**aistoryteller.team@gmail.com**.
